# Product roadmap 2026 — the three bets

Shared with the whole company in January. Directional, not a contract; sequencing may shift, the bets should not.

## Why these three

2025 taught us our growth limits are trust-shaped: customers who trust the substitute, trust the charge, and trust the clock reorder at multiples of those who were burned once. So 2026 is deliberately unflashy — three bets that each remove a top reason for a first bad experience.

<!-- frag: roadmap-three-bets | weight: helpful | topics: strategy -->
**Bet 1 — bring back saved cards, properly.** Card checkout friction is our biggest solvable drop-off, and the 2024 attempt is abandoned, not dead: this time built on provider tokens per the current [payments design](03-payments-design.md), with the fraud rules already agreed in [fraud rules](20-fraud-rules.md). **Bet 2 — make substitutions so good people stop dreading them.** Smarter automatic suggestions on top of the [substitution spec](08-substitutions-spec.md)'s ladder and hard lines — the rules stay, the picking of candidates gets better. **Bet 3 — protect the two-hour promise in Indore** through capacity, per the [offsite decision](13-offsite-notes-2025-delivery-debate.md): rider hiring, peak-hour batching improvements, festival planning from the [BRD's operating realities](14-brd-indore-expansion.md). Anyone joining a team this year is joining one of these three stories.
<!-- /frag -->

## What we said no to

A fourth city (density before geography). Instant 15-minute delivery (different business, different warehouses, not us). A subscription program (revisit 2027 once trust metrics justify it). Written down so the "why aren't we doing X" conversation starts from the reasons, not from zero.
