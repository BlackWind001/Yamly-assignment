# Welcome to GreenCart

GreenCart delivers groceries from local stores to people's homes. A customer opens our app, fills a cart, and we bring it to their door within two hours.

We started in Pune in 2021 with six partner stores. Today we work with about 200 stores across three cities: Pune (since 2021), Jaipur (since 2023), and Indore (since January 2025).

## How we are different

We do not own warehouses and we do not own the groceries. The stores do.

<!-- frag: model-pickers-in-stores | weight: helpful | topics: company-model -->
What we own is the experience. Our own trained pickers work inside the partner stores. When an order comes in, a GreenCart picker walks the aisles of that store, picks the items, weighs the loose vegetables and fruits, packs the bag, and hands it to a GreenCart rider. The store gives us shelf space and stock; we do everything the customer feels.
<!-- /frag -->

This model is cheaper than building warehouses, but it means we depend on store systems that we do not control. A lot of our engineering effort goes into living with that honestly. You will see this theme again and again in our documents.

## The promise

We promise delivery within two hours. That number is a brand promise, not just an operations number. Customers plan their cooking around it. Protecting it shapes many decisions you will read about, including some that look strange at first.
