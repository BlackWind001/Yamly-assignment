# Support playbook — refunds and angry moments

For support agents. Short scripts, clear limits, no guessing.

## What you can do yourself

Refunds and credits up to **₹500** per case: you decide, you issue, you note the reason code. Above ₹500: create a fraud-team approval ticket — this is a hard rule from the [refunds policy](04-refunds-policy.md), and telling the customer "it needs one approval, usually within a few hours" works better than pretending you can do it.

## The complaints you will actually see

<!-- frag: support-top-complaint-substitutes | weight: helpful | topics: substitutions, support -->
The number one complaint category, every single month, is **"I didn't want this substitute."** Read the case before responding: if the picker followed the [substitution ladder](08-substitutions-spec.md) and the customer accepted in the app, gently walk them through where they accepted it, then offer credits as goodwill if they are still unhappy. If the ladder was **not** followed — wrong kind of product, or anything crossing the veg/non-veg line — apologize without defending, refund fully and immediately, and flag the case with the "sub-rule-broken" code; those flags go straight to the product team weekly and are taken seriously.
<!-- /frag -->

Second place is weight-difference confusion ("you charged a different amount"): explain the estimate-then-weigh flow in one line — the [weight items note](09-weight-items-pricing.md) has the wording — and point at the automatic credit they already received. Third is late delivery: credits scaled by lateness, per the refunds policy table.

## Tone rules

Never blame the store or the picker to the customer; it is all GreenCart to them. Never promise a bank refund timeline faster than 5 working days even though it is usually 3. And never, ever argue about a veg/non-veg case — full refund, sincere apology, escalate.
