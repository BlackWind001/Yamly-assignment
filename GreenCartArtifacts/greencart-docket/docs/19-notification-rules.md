# Notification rules — when we may disturb a customer

We treat a notification as knocking on someone's door. Few rules, but hard ones.

<!-- frag: quiet-hours | weight: helpful | topics: notifications -->
**Quiet hours are 10 pm to 8 am IST. No SMS and no push notifications in that window — with exactly one exception: "your rider is arriving."** If someone places a late order, they are awake and waiting for it; every other message (offers, credits posted, order-delivered summaries, ratings nudges) queues until 8 am. There is no VIP override and no campaign override; marketing tools enforce it and so should anything new that sends messages. One angry "you woke my baby at midnight for a coupon" review costs more than any campaign earns.
<!-- /frag -->

During checkout and active delivery, in-app updates are unrestricted — the person is looking at the app. The three-minute substitution prompt ([substitution spec](08-substitutions-spec.md)) rides this exception during late orders: it is part of an active delivery the customer is awake for.
