# Refunds and credits — the rules

Money going back to a customer takes one of two shapes: **wallet credits** (instant, spendable on GreenCart) or a **bank refund** (3–5 working days, real money back). Customers strongly prefer credits for small amounts and real refunds for large ones. Our rules follow that.

## Automatic, no human involved

<!-- frag: weight-credit-auto-100 | weight: helpful | topics: refunds, weight-items -->
Weight differences and price differences up to **₹100** are returned as wallet credits automatically, the moment picking completes. No ticket, no approval, no delay. This covers the everyday case: the tomatoes weighed a little less than estimated. Customers see it as "GreenCart is honest by default," and it removes thousands of tiny support tickets a month.
<!-- /frag -->

## Needs a human

<!-- frag: refund-approval-500 | weight: helpful | topics: refunds, fraud -->
Any single refund above **₹500** — credits or bank — must be approved by the fraud team before it goes out. No exceptions, including for support leads. This rule came from a real abuse pattern in 2023 where a small group of accounts farmed "missing item" refunds. Below ₹500, trained support agents can issue refunds directly using the [support playbook](17-support-playbook-refunds.md).
<!-- /frag -->

## What kind, when

Missing item or wrong item: customer chooses credits or bank refund. Late delivery beyond the promise: credits, scaled by how late. Weight difference: always credits (automatic, above). Full order cancellation before picking: card holds are simply released (no money ever moved); UPI payments are refunded to the bank.

Every refund carries a reason code. Finance reads these monthly; the codes also feed the refunds telemetry file used by the product team.
