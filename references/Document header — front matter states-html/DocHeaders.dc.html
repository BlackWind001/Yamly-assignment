Field Required Shown as
titleYesSerif title; also the doc name under each search result.
statusYesFirst item in the meta row: green “Active” dot, or an amber “Stale” / red “Abandoned” pill. Stale and abandoned also tag the search result.
last_updatedYesExact date plus age (“2 years ago”); month and year on search results.

authorNoInitials, name, then role from the brackets. No author: the byline is hidden.
