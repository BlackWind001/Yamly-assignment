Search

Everything in the search pane. Add .has-results to .lib-search-pane after the first search: the bar slides to the top, the heading and hint fold away, and results fade in. The column centres itself while the pane is wide and pins to a 28px gutter when it narrows.

Search block

Eyebrow, serif heading, search bar and hint, positioned in the pane. Before a search it sits in the middle; with .has-results it moves to the top and the heading and hint collapse. Previews show the block in place (static) without the absolute positioning.
